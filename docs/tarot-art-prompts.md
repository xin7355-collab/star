# 塔羅牌圖 AI 繪圖提示詞（78 張＋牌背）

用 ChatGPT、Gemini、Midjourney 這類 AI 畫自己的牌，再到 App「占卜 → 塔羅 → 牌組 → 逐張換圖」換上去。

- 場景依 1909 年偉特牌（公有領域）的傳統象徵，提示詞文字是本專案自己寫的。
- 請 AI **不要寫字**：牌名由 App 蓋在牌面下方（AI 寫中文常寫錯）。
- 比例用直式 2:3（Midjourney 在最後加 `--ar 2:3`）。App 會裁成 3:5，主體放中間就不會被切到。
- 不要在提示詞加畫家、動畫公司或角色名稱；用你有權使用的 AI 服務（看它的使用條款）。用付費額度時先設預算上限。
- 第一張用「完整版」畫出喜歡的風格；之後在**同一個對話**用「短版」，風格比較一致。

## 共用風格（完整版已經含在每張裡）

```
Vertical tarot card illustration, portrait orientation, 2:3 aspect ratio, full-bleed artwork with no border and no frame. Ornate anime-inspired fantasy art: cel-shaded original characters with expressive eyes, elegant flowing costumes, intricate gold filigree and gemstone details woven into the scene, luminous magical lighting with sparkles and soft bloom, rich jewel tones of midnight indigo, gold, crimson and emerald, detailed painterly background, one consistent style for a full 78-card deck. Keep the main subject centered with a little margin on every side, and keep the bottom 12% of the image calm and simple because a name plate will be placed there. No text, no letters, no numbers written as text, no logo, no watermark, no signature. Original characters only; do not imitate any specific artist, studio or existing character. All figures fully clothed and wholesome.
```

## 短版的開頭（同一個對話接著畫時用）

```
Next card of the same deck. Keep exactly the same art style, palette, lighting and rules as the previous image (vertical 2:3, full-bleed, no border, no text, bottom 12% calm).
```
